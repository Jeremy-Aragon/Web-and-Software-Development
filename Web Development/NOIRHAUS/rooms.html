House Policies

Before you
arrive

We keep things simple. The only rules at Noir Haus are the ones that make your stay better — for you and for those sharing the building.

Check-In
From 3:00 PM
Early check-in from 12 PM subject to availability, at no charge. Penthouse and Black Label guests may arrange any arrival time.
Check-Out
Until 12:00 PM
Late check-out until 2 PM available at no charge, subject to availability. Guaranteed late checkout available on request.
Cancellation
72 Hours Prior
Full refund for cancellations made 72 hours before arrival. Penthouse & Black Label have a 7-day policy. Special rates are non-refundable.
Pets
Welcome
Dogs and cats welcome in all room categories. A pet amenity kit is provided on arrival. Please advise at booking so we can prepare accordingly.
Children
Under 12 · Complimentary
Children under 12 stay free in existing bedding. A crib or rollaway is available on request. The hotel's atmosphere is curated — we appreciate your guidance of younger guests.
Privacy
Unconditional
Guest identity, stay dates, and preferences are never disclosed. Our staff have signed permanent non-disclosure agreements. What happens at Noir Haus, stays here.
