VAULT
Modern American. Obsessively Sourced.

Housed in a converted bank vault two floors below street level, VAULT is the most awarded restaurant in Manhattan's hotel dining scene. Chef de cuisine Marcus Ohl leads a team of twelve, producing a nightly tasting menu that begins in the Hudson Valley and ends somewhere entirely unfamiliar.

The space — all burnished bronze and original vault architecture — seats forty-two. Reservations are released thirty days in advance and fill within hours. Walk-ins are not accepted.

The Jazz Bar
UMBRA
Shadows, Smoke & Single Malts.

Named for the darkest part of a shadow, UMBRA is the late-night soul of Noir Haus. A velvet-and-leather lounge on the hotel's second floor, open nightly from 9PM to 4AM. No dress code but a standard — arrive looking like you meant it.

Resident musicians perform Wednesday through Sunday. The stage is intimate — six feet from the nearest banquette. The bar carries 280 single malts, an archive of vintage Armagnac, and a cocktail programme that has been called, by more than one critic, the best in the city.

Private Events

The room
that remembers
every secret.

The Black Label Private Dining Room adjoins VAULT and seats up to eighteen guests. Accessible only through a separate entrance from the hotel lobby, it is used for business dinners, intimate celebrations, and occasions that require — and deserve — complete discretion.
