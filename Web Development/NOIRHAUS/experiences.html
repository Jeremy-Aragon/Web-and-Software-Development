Our Philosophy

The city is
the amenity.

your access.

Most hotels offer experiences the way they offer toiletries — in bulk, for everyone, designed to offend nobody. Noir Haus operates differently. Every experience we offer is shaped by a conversation with your concierge, refined to your tastes, and executed by people who have spent years building the access that money alone cannot buy.

"We don't have a list. We have a network. Tell us what you've never done, and we'll build the evening around that."

— Isabelle Voss, Director of Guest Experience

The Black Label Programme

Some experiences
have no price.
They have a name.

The Black Label Programme is the hotel's most private tier. Available to guests in The Black Label suite and to a limited number of annual members, it offers access to experiences that are never listed, never repeated, and never discussed in advance.

Each experience is built entirely around a conversation between you and our Director of Guest Experience. The structure, timing, and people involved are disclosed only to you. What we can say is that no two Black Label evenings have ever been the same.

  • Private access to events and openings before public announcement
  • Dedicated concierge number — answered by a named individual, 24 hours
  • Priority access to all nine signature experiences, confirmed same-day
  • Quarterly curated delivery to your home address — wherever in the world
  • Annual Black Label dinner with the hotel's founding collective

The Process

How every
evening begins.

Nothing at Noir Haus is off the shelf. Every experience begins with a conversation and is shaped around the specific nature of your stay, your party, and the kind of evening you're unwilling to leave to chance.

01

The Conversation

You reach your concierge by phone, message, or in person. You describe what you want — however vague — or simply name the feeling you're after. We take it from there.

02

The Design

Within two hours, your concierge returns with a proposal — structured around your dates, party size, and preferences. Timing, people, transport, and every detail accounted for.

03

The Arrangement

We make every booking, arrange every car, brief every participant. You receive a single confirmation. You bring nothing. You carry nothing. You simply arrive.

04

The Evening

Your concierge is reachable throughout. Any adjustment, any addition — handled without interruption to the experience itself. The night is yours to inhabit entirely.
