The Hotel

A building that
chose its guests

Noir Haus was not built to serve everyone. It was built for those who feel most alive after midnight, who appreciate the weight of good silence, and who understand that true luxury is never announced.

Forty-two floors. Each one designed by a different artist from the Noir Haus collective, each floor a different chapter. The architecture is brutalist. The interiors are baroque. The collision is intentional.

Founded in 2019 by a trio of architects, gallerists, and former hospitality renegades — Noir Haus remains privately held, deliberately small, and uncompromisingly itself.

"A building with a soul."
— Architectural Digest, 2024
