How It Began

Born from a belief
that beauty
belongs in everyday life.

MODRN STIL began in 2019 in a small apartment in Quezon City, where our founder Ana Reyes found herself frustrated by a simple problem: she wanted her home to feel intentional — calm, curated, and deeply personal — but could not find pieces that balanced honest craftsmanship with modern restraint.

So she started sourcing them herself. First from local artisans in Pampanga and Cebu. Then from small studios in Japan and Scandinavia. Every piece chosen not for trend, but for how it would feel to live with it for ten years.

What began as a personal obsession became a small online shop, then a community, then MODRN STIL — a brand built entirely on the philosophy that your home should tell your story, not someone else's.

What We Stand For

Our guiding principles

01
Intentional Curation
Every single piece in our collection is hand-selected. We say no to hundreds of products for every one we say yes to. If we wouldn't put it in our own homes, it doesn't belong in yours.
02
Honest Craftsmanship
We partner only with makers who share our commitment to slow, careful work. Natural materials, traditional methods, and the kind of quality that gets better with age — not worse.
03
Quiet Sustainability
We don't shout about sustainability because we think it should be the baseline, not the headline. Responsible sourcing, minimal packaging, and pieces made to last decades — not seasons.
04
Community First
Over 60% of our pieces come from Filipino artisans. We believe the most beautiful things come from people who pour their heritage into their work — and we exist to help them reach the world.
05
Timelessness Over Trend
We release new collections slowly and deliberately. We'd rather you love something for twenty years than need to replace it in two. Beauty that endures is the only beauty worth designing for.
06
Radical Honesty
We tell you where things are made, how long they take, and what they're worth. No inflated markups, no mystery. We believe trust is the most valuable thing a brand can earn.
Our Journey

Six years of slow, steady growth

2019
The Beginning
Ana Reyes launches MODRN STIL from her apartment with 12 hand-sourced pieces. The first collection sells out in 72 hours, entirely through word of mouth and a single Instagram post.
2020
The Studio Opens
We open a small studio showroom in Quezon City. During the pandemic, we pivot to virtual home consultations — and discover our community is hungry for guidance, not just products.
2021
The Artisan Program
We launch our Artisan Partnership Program, formalizing relationships with 18 Filipino craftspeople across Pampanga, Cebu, and Ilocos. Their names appear on every piece they make.
2023
International Expansion
MODRN STIL ships its first international orders to Singapore, Japan, and Australia. We're featured in Architectural Digest Philippines and Wallpaper* Magazine's Southeast Asia edition.
2025
8,000 Homes & Counting
We celebrate our pieces living in over 8,000 homes across 14 countries. We open a flagship studio in BGC with a permanent gallery, workshop space, and weekly styling consultations.
2026
Today
350+ curated pieces. 40+ artisan partners. One unwavering belief: that the most beautiful home is the one that feels most like you. We're just getting started.
The Making Of

Crafted by hands.
Chosen with intention.

Each piece in the MODRN STIL collection begins with a conversation — with a maker, a material, a place. We visit studios. We feel the wood grain. We ask hard questions about process, provenance, and permanence.

Our team travels to source new pieces no more than twice a year. We take our time. We turn things down. The result is a collection where every single object has earned its place.
